Applied 50 times and heard nothing?

It's usually a filter, not a verdict on you. Here's how that filter works, and how to get past it.

What's "the filter"?

  1. 1Many companies put every CV into hiring software, called an ATS (applicant tracking system).
  2. 2The software looks for the skills and words from the job ad, then ranks the CVs.
  3. 3Recruiters usually open only the CVs near the top, and skim them fast.

So if your CV doesn't use the job ad's words, or hides your results, it can be skipped before a person reads it. This check shows you where that's happening.
