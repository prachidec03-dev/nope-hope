Refunds & cancellation

If something goes wrong, you get your money back

Last updated: 17 October 2026

Full refunds

You get a full refund if:

  • you were charged but didn't receive your report
  • you were charged twice for the same purchase
  • a technical problem on our side stopped the report from working

Write to us within 7 days of the payment with your registered mobile number or email and the payment ID from your Razorpay receipt.

When refunds don't apply

Reports are digital and delivered instantly, so we can't refund a report you've already received just because you disagree with the feedback. If you think a report is wrong, tell us anyway; we'll look into it and may offer a free report instead.

Cancellation

There are no subscriptions, so there's nothing to cancel. The 30-day unlimited plan ends on its own. You can delete your account at any time from the contact page.

How long refunds take

Approved refunds are made to your original payment method within 5–7 working days. Your bank may take a little longer to show it.
